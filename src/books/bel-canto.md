Ann Pratchett’s award winning, _New York Times *bestselling *Bel Canto *balances themes of love and crisis as disparate characters learn that music is their only common language. As in Patchett’s other novels, including *Truth & Beauty _and _The Magician’s Assistant_, the author’s lyrical prose and lucid imagination make *Bel Canto *a captivating story of strength and frailty, love and imprisonment, and an inspiring tale of transcendent romance.
